# Never block on the human

Apply when you are tempted to ask whether to do something reversible.

The human supervises asynchronously.
Make the reasonable decision, proceed, show the result and let the human correct course.

- Do the reversible work, then explain what you chose and why.
- Irreversible actions still need confirmation: a force-push to a shared branch, deleting data, a deploy, a message to someone outside the session.
- The Well-Engineering hard rules win over this principle.
  Installing packages, changing files under `$HOME` or changing a machine's configuration needs explicit approval in the current conversation.
- Product direction comes from the human, execution does not wait on them.
